# Dell Inspiron 15 — Quick Start Manual

## In the Box

Dell Inspiron 15 laptop, 65 W USB-C power adapter with cable, quick start guide and safety and warranty information.

## First Setup

Connect the power adapter and press the power button. Follow the Windows 11 setup screens to connect to Wi-Fi and sign in with a Microsoft account. Install all Windows updates and Dell Update drivers before installing other software.

## Ports

Two USB 3.2 Type-A ports, one USB-C port with power delivery and DisplayPort, HDMI 1.4, an SD card reader and a 3.5 mm headset jack.

## Battery Care

The 54 Wh battery lasts up to 8 hours of mixed use. To extend battery life, enable Dell Power Manager's Adaptive mode, which limits charging to 80 percent when the laptop is mostly plugged in.

## Upgrades

RAM is upgradable to 32 GB using two SO-DIMM slots, and the M.2 2230/2280 SSD slot accepts NVMe drives. Opening the laptop does not void the warranty, but damage caused during an upgrade is not covered, and laptops with customer-installed hardware changes cannot be returned.

## Factory Reset

Before returning or servicing the laptop, back up your data and use Settings > System > Recovery > Reset this PC > Remove everything.
