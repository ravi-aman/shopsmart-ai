# ShopSmart Return Policy

## Overview

Most items bought on ShopSmart can be returned for a refund or replacement within the return window for their category. The return window starts on the delivery date shown in Orders. Category-specific policies (such as the Laptop Return Policy) take precedence over this general policy.

## Return Windows by Category

- Electronics (phones, tablets, audio, accessories): 7 days from delivery.
- Laptops: see the Laptop Return Policy.
- Fashion, footwear, bags and jewellery: 30 days from delivery, unworn with tags attached.
- Home appliances and furniture: 10 days from delivery, for damaged, defective or wrong items only.
- Books: 10 days from delivery, in unread condition.
- Healthcare devices: 7 days from delivery, unopened and with the seal intact.
- Groceries and fresh produce: not returnable; report damaged or expired items within 24 hours for a refund.

## Condition of Returned Items

Items must be returned with all original accessories, manuals, free gifts and packaging. Electronics must be reset to factory settings and removed from any account lock.

## Opened Products

Opened electronics can be returned within the category window only if they are undamaged and complete. Opened personal-care, beauty and fragrance products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Replacements

If a replacement is requested and the same item is out of stock, ShopSmart issues a full refund instead.

## Exchanges

Fashion and footwear can be exchanged once for a different size or colour of the same product within 30 days of delivery.
