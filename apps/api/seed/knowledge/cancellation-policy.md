# Order Cancellation Policy

## Before Shipment

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is shipped. Go to Orders, select the item and choose Cancel. The refund starts immediately after cancellation is confirmed.

## After Shipment

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the courier arrives, or accept it and request a return within the return window for that category. Refused deliveries are refunded after the item reaches the warehouse, usually within 5 to 7 days.

## Out for Delivery

Orders marked Out for Delivery cannot be cancelled or redirected. Refuse the delivery or return the item instead.

## Partial Cancellation

For orders with several items, each item that has not yet shipped can be cancelled separately.

## Cancellation by ShopSmart

ShopSmart may cancel an order if the item becomes unavailable, the payment fails verification, or the delivery address is not serviceable. You will be notified and refunded in full.
