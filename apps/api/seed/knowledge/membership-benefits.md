# ShopSmart Membership Benefits

## Membership Tiers

ShopSmart has three membership tiers: Basic (free for every account), Prime ($9.99 per month or $99 per year) and Premium ($19.99 per month or $199 per year). Members can upgrade at any time; the price difference is charged pro rata.

## Basic Benefits

Basic members get free Standard delivery on orders of $35 or more, standard customer support by chat and email, and access to all public sales.

## Prime Benefits

Prime membership includes free delivery: free Standard delivery on every order with no minimum order value, plus free One-Day delivery in metro cities. Prime members also get early access to lightning deals 30 minutes before other customers, an extra 5 percent back in ShopSmart Wallet on electronics, and priority chat support.

## Premium Benefits

Premium includes everything in Prime, plus free Same-Day delivery, instant refunds to the ShopSmart Wallet at pickup for orders up to $1,500, an extended 30-day return window on fashion and 14 days on electronics (excluding laptops), a dedicated support line answered within 2 minutes, and one free extended-warranty plan per year on any laptop or smartphone.

## Cancelling Membership

Paid memberships can be cancelled at any time from Account settings. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current billing month. Annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase are refunded in full if no paid benefit has been used.
