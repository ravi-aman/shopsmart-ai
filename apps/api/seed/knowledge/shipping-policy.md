# ShopSmart Shipping Policy

## Delivery Options

ShopSmart offers Standard delivery, One-Day delivery (metro cities only) and Same-Day delivery (selected metro pin codes, orders placed before 12 noon). Delivery estimates shown at checkout use the nearest fulfilment centre with stock.

## Shipping Fees for Basic Members

Basic members get free Standard delivery on orders of $35 or more. Orders below $35 pay a $4.99 Standard delivery fee. One-Day delivery costs $7.99 and Same-Day delivery costs $12.99.

## Shipping Fees for Prime Members

Prime members get free Standard delivery on all orders with no minimum order value, and free One-Day delivery in metro cities. Same-Day delivery costs $4.99 for Prime members.

## Shipping Fees for Premium Members

Premium members get free Standard, One-Day and Same-Day delivery on all orders with no minimum order value.

## Large Appliances

Refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners ship by scheduled delivery within 2 to 5 days, with free installation booked separately. Large appliances are not eligible for One-Day or Same-Day delivery.

## Delivery Attempts

The carrier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. If all attempts fail, the order is returned to the seller and refunded according to the Refund Policy.
