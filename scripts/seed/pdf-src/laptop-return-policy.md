# Laptop Return Policy

This policy applies to all laptops, notebooks, Chromebooks and 2-in-1 convertibles sold or fulfilled by ShopSmart. It adds to the general ShopSmart Return Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to laptops.

## Return Window

Unopened laptops in their original sealed packaging can be returned within 10 days of delivery for a full refund.

## Opened Products

Opened laptops can be returned within 7 days of delivery if there is no physical damage, liquid damage or missing accessories. The laptop must be reset to factory settings, signed out of all accounts (Windows, Apple ID, Google) and returned with its charger, cables and documentation.

Opened laptops that have been registered for an extended warranty or activated with a perpetual software licence bundle (for example Microsoft Office) are still returnable, but the bundled licence key will be deactivated.

A laptop that has been opened and used for more than 7 days is not returnable; defects after that point are handled under the manufacturer warranty.

## Damaged or Defective on Arrival

If a laptop arrives damaged or does not power on, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the packaging and device. ShopSmart will arrange a free replacement or full refund, whether or not the box has been opened.

## Non-Returnable Cases

Laptops with physical damage caused after delivery, broken screens, removed serial number labels or customer-installed hardware changes (for example replaced RAM or SSD) cannot be returned.

## How to Return

Start a return from Orders, choose the item and reason, and schedule a pickup. A quality check is performed at pickup and again at the warehouse. Refund timelines are described in the ShopSmart Refund Policy.
