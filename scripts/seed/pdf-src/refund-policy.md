# Refund Policy

This policy explains when and how ShopSmart refunds money for returned, cancelled or undelivered orders.

## When a Refund Starts

For returns, the refund is initiated within 48 hours after the item passes the quality check at pickup. For cancelled orders, the refund is initiated immediately after cancellation is confirmed. For orders lost in transit, the refund is initiated once the carrier confirms the loss, at most 7 days after the promised delivery date.

## Refund Timeline

After a refund is initiated, the time for money to reach you depends on the payment method:

ShopSmart Wallet: instant.
UPI: 1 to 3 business days.
Net banking: 3 to 5 business days.
Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days.
Cash on delivery: refunded to your bank account or ShopSmart Wallet within 5 to 7 business days of providing bank details.

## Partial Refunds

If a returned item is missing accessories, free gifts or original packaging, ShopSmart may deduct the value of the missing items from the refund. You will see the deduction and the reason before the refund is processed.

## Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are refunded when the return is due to a damaged, defective or wrong item. For change-of-mind returns, the original shipping fee is not refunded.

## Premium Members

Premium members receive refunds to the ShopSmart Wallet instantly at pickup, before the warehouse quality check, for orders up to $1,500.
