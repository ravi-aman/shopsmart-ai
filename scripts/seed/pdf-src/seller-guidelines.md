# Seller Guidelines

These guidelines apply to every third-party seller listing products on the ShopSmart marketplace.

## Listing Standards

Product titles must include brand, model and key variant (for example storage or colour). Specifications must match the manufacturer data sheet. Images must show the actual product on a white or neutral background with no watermarks or promotional text.

## Dispatch and Fulfilment

Sellers must dispatch orders within 2 business days of order confirmation. Orders not dispatched within 4 business days are automatically cancelled and count against the seller's performance score.

## Returns and Refunds

Sellers must accept returns that comply with the ShopSmart Return Policy and the category-specific return policies. Refunds for seller-fulfilled orders follow the ShopSmart Refund Policy timelines.

## Prohibited Products

Counterfeit goods, refurbished products listed as new, prescription medicines and products without valid safety certification may not be listed.

## Performance Standards

Sellers must keep the order defect rate below 1 percent and the late dispatch rate below 4 percent. Sellers above these limits may have listings suspended.
